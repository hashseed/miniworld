# Miniworld

A passive, historically accurate diorama of world history on a 3D globe, made for exploring history together with a child. Miniature people live era-typical lives on accurate continents, from the last ice age to the present day. The app runs in the browser on desktop and phones, in German and English.

The specification lives in [docs/spec](docs/spec/README.md) and is the source of truth for everything the app does.
