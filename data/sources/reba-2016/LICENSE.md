# Historical urban population licence

Reba, M., Reitsma, F. and Seto, K. C. (2016): Spatializing 6,000 years of global urbanization from 3700 BC to AD 2000. Scientific Data 3, 160034, https://doi.org/10.1038/sdata.2016.34. Data: figshare, https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.2059494 (Chandler, `chandlerV2.csv`), https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.2059497 (Modelski ancient, `modelskiAncientV2.csv`) and https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.2059500 (Modelski modern, `modelskiModernV2.csv`).

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/, as stated on each figshare record and for the article.

The original files list geocoded cities with their populations at dated years from 3700 BC to AD 2000, from Chandler (1987), Four Thousand Years of Urban Growth, and Modelski (2003), World Cities: -3000 to 2000. Kept here, made by `npm run simplify-data`: `cities.json`, every city once with its position, certainty and the year and size of its first dated population, the places where Neolithic villages are founded (spec neolithic/villages.md section 2). The original files are not stored; their URLs and checksums are in `data/sources.json`.
