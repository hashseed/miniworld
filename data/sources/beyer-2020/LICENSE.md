# Late Quaternary climate, bioclimate and vegetation licence

Beyer, R. M., Krapp, M. and Manica, A. (2020): High-resolution terrestrial climate, bioclimate and vegetation for the last 120,000 years. Scientific Data, 7, 236, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-020-0552-1. Data: figshare, https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.12293345 (file `LateQuaternary_Environment.nc`, v1.0).

Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/, as stated on the figshare record. Note that the repackaged copy distributed by the pastclim R package (Zenodo) is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 and must not be used.

The original file holds a 0.5 degree land grid (60°S to 90°N) with 72 time slices from 120,000 years ago to today, with monthly climate, the 19 bioclimatic variables, net primary production, leaf area index and BIOME4 biomes. Kept here, made by `npm run simplify-data`: `biome` (BIOME4 class), `BIO1` (annual mean temperature, tenths of a degree) and `BIO12` (annual precipitation, mm) for the slices from 54,000 years ago to today, as Brotli-compressed grids with JSON headers. The original file is not stored; its URL and checksum are in `data/sources.json`.
