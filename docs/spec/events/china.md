# Events: China

Dated events for China from the Shang dynasty (c. 1600 BCE) to the end of the timeline in December 1991, including the dynasties that ruled from China, Taiwan after 1949, and Tibet and Xinjiang where they relate to China. From 841 BCE, the first securely dated year in Chinese history, there is an event at least every 20 years; before that the list gives approximate dates for what the record holds. Dates come from general historical knowledge, are approximate where marked "c.", and are checked against sources during the data build. Natural disasters, epidemics and famines are in [events.md](../events.md), and the headline events from [events.md](../events.md) (Qin unifies China, the Silk Road, paper, Zheng He, the founding of the PRC) are not repeated here but count toward the 20-year rule. Every event has a stable id. Major wars and battles are in [wars.md](../wars.md) and are linked by id under "Linked wars and battles" below; smaller wars and battles of the region are listed here. The "Coordinates" column gives the latitude and longitude the camera flies to, as defined in [content-format.md](../content-format.md#4-place) section 4; a note in brackets marks a representative point for an event spread over an area, a route or several places.
