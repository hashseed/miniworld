# Events: Europe

Dated events for Europe and the Greek and Roman Mediterranean, including Byzantium, Russia and the Ottoman Empire in Europe, from the first Olympic Games in 776 BCE to the end of the timeline in December 1991. From 776 BCE there is an event at least every 20 years. Dates come from general historical knowledge, are approximate where marked "c.", and are checked against sources during the data build. Natural disasters, epidemics and famines are in [events.md](../events.md), and the headline events from [events.md](../events.md) (such as the founding of Rome, Marathon, the Parthenon, the sack of Rome, Charlemagne, Hastings, the Reformation, the French Revolution and the fall of the Berlin Wall) are not repeated here but count toward the 20-year rule. Every event has a stable id. Major wars and battles are in [wars.md](../wars.md) and are linked by id under "Linked wars and battles" below; smaller wars and battles of the region are listed here. The "Coordinates" column gives the latitude and longitude the camera flies to, as defined in [content-format.md](../content-format.md#4-place) section 4; a note in brackets marks a representative point for an event spread over an area, a route or several places.
