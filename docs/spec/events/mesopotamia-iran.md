# Events: Mesopotamia and Iran

Dated events for Mesopotamia (Sumer, Akkad, Babylon, Assyria) and Iran (Elam, the Medes, the Achaemenids, Seleucids, Parthians and Sasanians, Islamic Iran, the Safavids, Qajars and Pahlavis, and the Islamic Republic) from c. 3100 BCE to the end of the timeline in December 1991, including Iraq after Mesopotamia stops being the centre of its own empires. From 911 BCE, when the Assyrian lists of yearly officials (eponyms) give a secure date for every year, there is an event at least every 20 years; before that the list gives approximate dates for what the record holds. Dates come from general historical knowledge, are approximate where marked "c.", and are checked against sources during the data build. Dates before 1500 BCE follow the middle chronology, which is debated. Natural disasters, epidemics and famines are in [events.md](../events.md), and the headline events from [events.md](../events.md) (writing at Uruk, the Code of Hammurabi, Marathon, Alexander's campaigns, the Silk Road, the early spread of Islam) are not repeated here but count toward the 20-year rule. Every event has a stable id. Major wars and battles are in [wars.md](../wars.md) and are linked by id under "Linked wars and battles" below; smaller wars and battles of the region are listed here. The "Coordinates" column gives the latitude and longitude the camera flies to, as defined in [content-format.md](../content-format.md#4-place) section 4; a note in brackets marks a representative point for an event spread over an area, a route or several places.
