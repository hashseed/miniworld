# Famous people

The first list of famous people, in order of birth, spread across the world and across history. Each appears as a unique figure at the listed places during the active part of their life and fades out when it ends. Names are the English forms; every name is localised. Dates are approximate where marked "c." and are checked against sources during the data build.
